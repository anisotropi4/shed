# Collaborative Research Institute (CRI)

The *Collaborative Research Institute (CRI)* is an organisation with private IT facilities for research and development. The Institute is a not-for-profit private organisation as the operational division of the *Collaborative Research Foundation (CRF)*. We are not associated with any business, anywhere. We have been operating for several decades and we own our domain names.

The *Collaborative Research Institute (CRI)* maintains private knowledgebases based on our research programmes that are used by stream leads to guide our research.

The *Collaborative Research Institute* exists to promote progress in computing technology and is privately funded. We are grateful to collaborators who contribute knowledge to the Collaborative Research Institute.

The *Collaborative Research Institute* uses the Collaborative Research private cloud for advanced systems computer research and prototyping.

## Supporting Research
Research time on the CRI systems is allotted to authorised users. Priority is given by merit, ethics and availability of the facility. As an example, individual Research & Development for medical advances (pharma, bio), Multi-Science research (new concepts) and others.
